# Asset license boundaries

The MIT license in LICENSE covers the project's original software and documentation. It does not relicense third-party media.

- Kenney base meshes and their derived alpha assets retain CC0 1.0. Original added character details, textures, rig animations, tool meshes, and icon are offered under CC0 1.0.
- MakeHuman Community / MPFB core assets used for the six new Alpha 0.3 bodies retain CC0 1.0, with source and export rights recorded in assets/provenance/v03-human-assets.md. The project's outfits, hair, PBR materials and rig-animation adaptations are CC0 1.0. This does not relicense the upstream MakeHuman/MPFB software.
- The six original Alpha 0.3 urban models and generated PBR surfaces are CC0 1.0, with the declaration in assets/provenance/v03-urban-CC0.txt. Government webpages were visual/design references only; their photos, branding and GIS data are not redistributed in the game.
- The original bicycle model and its derived Blender/GLB copies retain CC BY 3.0, with the attribution and modification notice in CREDITS.md.
- Noto Sans TC Regular and its renamed Seven District Sans TC game-text subset are SIL OFL 1.1, with the complete license in godot/assets/fonts/OFL.txt. The original remains as source; the Web package includes the renamed subset. Both retain the original font copyright and their own license.
- Original generated .wav feedback clips are CC BY 4.0, credited to Seven District contributors. Their generator source is MIT.
- The bundled Godot executable and third-party runtime components retain the licenses in assets/provenance/Godot-LICENSE.txt and Godot-COPYRIGHT.txt.

The 70-item planning register is a future production inventory. Its entries marked Proposed / pending provenance are not representations that those models exist or have acquired final licenses. The source repository contains 25 original alpha model exports and 12 added Alpha 0.3 models; their actual source rights are recorded in the original and v03 provenance ledgers.
